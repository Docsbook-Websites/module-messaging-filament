# Ecommerce

[liberusoftware/ecommerce-laravel](https://github.com/liberusoftware/ecommerce-laravel) is an online shop system built on Laravel 13, PHP 8.5, Filament 5 and Livewire 4 — positioned as an alternative to Magento, WooCommerce, Shopify and CubeCart for teams that want to own their storefront and data.
